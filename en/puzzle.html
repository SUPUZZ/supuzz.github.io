How to play Puzzle Online

The rules are simple, and there is no timer to beat unless you want one.

  1. 1

    Pick a picture

    Choose a snow garden, sea creatures, winter garden or coral reef picture. You can also use a photo from your own device.

  2. 2

    Choose the difficulty

    Start with 9 pieces, then work up to 16, 25, 36 or 49. Younger builders usually begin with the 3×3 grid.

  3. 3

    Drag the pieces

    Drag a piece next to its neighbour. When the edges match, the pieces snap together and move as one group.

  4. 4

    Finish the picture

    Keep joining groups until the whole picture is back. The timer, move counter and remaining group count are shown above the board.

A screen-time activity that still builds skills

Picture puzzles ask children to match colour, shape and edge details. That is the same kind of visual thinking they use when they sort blocks by size or find the piece that completes a build.

Dragging pieces on a phone or tablet also practises finger control and hand-eye coordination. Because a round can be short, the puzzle works well as a five-minute break between other activities.

The pictures double as conversation starters. Name the animals in the snow garden, count the sea creatures in the reef, or describe the colours in the rainbow blocks while you play together.

Puzzle Online questions

Is Puzzle Online free to play?

Yes. Every puzzle on this page is free, with no sign-up, no download and no in-game purchases.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Puzzle Online runs in a modern web browser on a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop computer.

How many pieces can I choose?

You can play 3×3 (9 pieces), 4×4 (16), 5×5 (25), 6×6 (36) or 7×7 (49). Difficulty only changes the number of pieces, so you can replay the same picture at a harder level.

Can I use my own photo?

Yes. Choose “Use my own photo” in the picture picker. The image is processed in your browser and is never uploaded to our servers.

Are the pictures from SUPUZZ building sets?

Yes. The collections are based on SUPUZZ snow garden, coral reef, dinosaur and rainbow building sets. Follow the links under each collection to see the matching toy.

Does the puzzle work on a phone?

Yes. Drag pieces with one finger. On a small screen we suggest starting with 9 or 16 pieces.

What are snow garden and winter garden stacking toys?

They are SUPUZZ building sets with snowy bricks, trees and Arctic animal figures. The snow garden stacking toy and winter garden stacking set toy pictures in Puzzle Online use the same world, so children can rebuild the scene in their hands.

Take the puzzle off the screen

If your child enjoys rebuilding the snow garden or coral reef, the matching building set lets them create the scene in their hands. SUPUZZ sets use chunky, colourful pieces that are easy for small fingers to connect.
