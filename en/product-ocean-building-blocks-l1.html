BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few useful answers.

How do the three levels compare?

Coral Reef L1 has 38 pieces, L2 has 76 and L3 has 136. The L2 and L3 sets include storage boxes.

Does this version include a storage box?

A storage box is not listed for L1. The 76-piece L2 and 136-piece L3 sets include one.

Can I add to the set later?

Yes. It belongs to SUPUZZ’s compatible nature-themed building collection. Additional ocean, ice and forest sets are sold separately.
