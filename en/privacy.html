Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Overview

This policy explains what personal information SUPUZZ collects when you visit supuzz.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. SUPUZZ is a brand website for building toys and kids furniture; product purchases take place on Amazon.com and are governed by Amazon's own policies.

Information we collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and the language of the page you subscribed from. When you send a message through the contact form, we collect the name, email address and details you provide.

We also collect anonymous usage data, such as pages visited, approximate time spent and a randomly generated visitor identifier, to understand how the site is used.

How we use information

We use your email address to send the newsletter you requested, including play ideas, new-product news and special offers, and to respond to your inquiries. Anonymous usage data is used in aggregate to improve the website.

Cookies and local storage

The site may store a small language-preference cookie and a visitor identifier in your browser's local storage. These help us keep the site in your preferred language and measure aggregate usage. They do not identify you personally.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the website and deliver emails (for example, a newsletter platform), only as needed to provide those services. Links to Amazon.com are provided for purchases; information you provide to Amazon is subject to Amazon's privacy policy.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send. To access, correct or delete your information, or to ask a question, contact us using the address below.

Children

This website is intended for adults, such as parents and educators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
