Connect, imagine and play together with SUPUZZ Rainbow Waffle Blocks

SUPUZZ Editorial Team · Published November 20, 2025 · 4 min read

What shall we build? Colorful interlocking pieces give children a place to start. Follow an example, change it halfway through or invent something new. The process is part of the play.

From flat shapes to a world in three dimensions

Connect pieces sideways, then build upward. Start with a small wall or enclosure and turn it into a house or vehicle. Take it apart and try a different arrangement; finishing a model is only one way to play.

Discover color, shape and balance

STEAM brings science, technology, engineering, art and mathematics together. Everyday questions such as “Will it stand?” invite children to observe and try again. Give them time to explain their ideas.

  • Sort pieces by color and arrange a favorite sequence.
  • Turn a flat shape into a standing construction.
  • Change the base when a tall build falls over.
  • Give a finished creation a name and a story.

More than one thing to build

Vehicles, cameras and small playgrounds are starting points. Ask what could change, then make the design your own.

Ideas grow when we play together

One person builds a base while another chooses colors. Children can take different roles and join at their own pace.

PLAY WITH ALL KIDS. We believe play is a universal language of discovery: a way to imagine, build and connect.

Make cleanup the start of the next adventure

Gather the pieces after play. Sorting by color can be part of tidying, and a photo can save a favorite creation before rebuilding. Storage cases and accessories vary by set; check the selected product.

At home or in the classroom

Try a shared project such as a bridge, or allow time for free building first. For gifts and group activities, check the selected product’s age guidance, piece sizes, contents and instructions.
