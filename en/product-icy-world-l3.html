BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few useful answers.

How do the three levels compare?

Icy World L1 has 40 pieces, L2 has 79 and L3 has 136. The L2 and L3 sets include storage boxes.

Does it include the storage box?

Yes. The L3 package-content image shows the storage box as part of the set.

Can it be combined with the ocean collection?

Yes. SUPUZZ’s nature-themed stems, decorative pieces and bases are designed to mix across the forest, ocean and ice collections. Additional sets are sold separately.
