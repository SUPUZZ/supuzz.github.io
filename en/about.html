Our Story

A few building pieces can become a whole world.

At SUPUZZ, we believe play has the power to bring kids together.

A few building pieces can become a garden, an ocean, a dinosaur world, or something no one has imagined before. A simple idea can turn into a shared adventure. And when kids play together, they create more than things—they create moments, connections, and memories.

Good to Know

Questions families often ask.

What does SUPUZZ make?

SUPUZZ creates building toys, kids furniture, and playful experiences. The range includes rainbow and waffle building blocks, Ocean World coral reef sets, Ice World sets, Forest & Dinosaur World sets, and kids tables and chairs.

What ages are SUPUZZ toys designed for?

Each product lists its own age recommendation. Waffle building blocks are designed for ages 2–8, many themed building sets are recommended for ages 3 and up, and our kids furniture is sized for children ages 1–4.

Are SUPUZZ building toys open-ended?

Yes. The pieces are designed to connect, stack, mix, and rebuild. Kids can follow a themed idea or invent their own world, and sets can be combined for bigger builds.

Where can I buy SUPUZZ toys?

You can browse the full range on the SUPUZZ website and shop through our Amazon store. For bulk orders, schools, and retail enquiries, contact us directly.

Does SUPUZZ offer digital play?

Yes. SUPUZZ Puzzle Online is a free browser puzzle that turns our toy worlds into jigsaw pictures, with no download required.
