Before you give building blocks: a five-point family checklist

SUPUZZ Editorial Team · Published April 17, 2026

A good building gift fits everyday life as well as the moment the box opens. Look beyond box size and piece count to the child’s interests and the family’s space.

Five things to check before ordering

If possible, ask a parent or caregiver about these points.

  1. Age guidance: does the actual product match the child and its instructions?
  2. Interests: does it connect with favorite vehicles, animals or color play?
  3. Play space: is there room to spread out and build?
  4. Storage: is a case included, and is there somewhere to keep it?
  5. Contents: can you distinguish example photos from the pieces and accessories included?

Cleanup is part of the gift

A place to gather the pieces makes the next play session easier to start. Check the case and storage space; packaging differs between products and versions.

Add an invitation to build together

Let’s build a home for your car. A simple invitation can be more useful than a perfect first model. Sit alongside the child and leave room for their idea.

A level name is not an age label

L1, L2 and L3 identify versions. Contents and age guidance differ across collections, so check the selected product rather than applying one age chart to every set.

Plan seasonal and group gifts early

Share your quantity, budget and preferred timing when asking about a group order. Confirm current price, stock and delivery when buying. Gift orders do not automatically receive an education discount.

Frequently asked questions

Can I give blocks for a second birthday?

Check the specific product’s age guidance and pieces. Products from one brand can have different age recommendations.

Is the set with the most pieces always best?

No. Consider the theme, play space and cleanup as well as the number of pieces.
