Thanksgiving Giving 2026: Lisa’s Toys to Kenya

By The SUPUZZ Team · October 8, 2026 · 5 min read

Lisa once worked in Kenya. Today, from the United States, she sends toys to Kenya. Those two facts tell a small but meaningful story: returning home does not have to mean leaving every connection behind.

As Thanksgiving approaches, her story offers a starting point for families looking for Thanksgiving giving ideas for kids. Think of someone you still care about, even if distance has changed the way you keep in touch. Then consider what would be welcome in their everyday life.

U.S. Thanksgiving 2026 falls on Thursday, November 26. The date is listed in the U.S. Office of Personnel Management’s holiday calendar. That makes October a useful time to start a thoughtful giving plan, especially when a gift needs to cross borders.

From working in Kenya to sending toys from America

For Lisa, Kenya is a place where she has worked. Her connection began with time spent there, and now it continues through toys sent from the United States.

A move changes the practical details of a relationship. You can no longer stop by in person. Sharing something requires planning. A gift has to travel the distance that you once traveled yourself. Lisa’s story brings that distance into focus through an everyday object: a toy.

It is a fitting story to reflect on at Thanksgiving. Many of us have people or places woven into our lives that are absent from the holiday table. Remembering them can take a practical form—a call, a letter, an invitation, or a gift chosen with their needs in mind.

Lisa sends toys to Kenya. For readers considering their own way to give, the question is personal: what connection would you like to keep caring for this year?

What makes a Thanksgiving gift for kids meaningful?

A meaningful gift begins with the child who will receive it. What do they enjoy? What materials do they already have? Would a caregiver prefer something for quiet play, outdoor games, drawing, or building together?

A toy can offer an invitation to do something: invent a character, draw a picture, build a scene, or share a game. The useful part of choosing is imagining how the recipient will actually use it, rather than selecting something only because it looks impressive in a box.

5 Thanksgiving giving ideas for families in 2026

Use Lisa’s story as an invitation to make your own plan. A thoughtful family activity can be small enough to finish and specific enough to be useful.

  1. Choose a connection to nurture. Think about a friend far away, a relative you rarely see, or a community organization you already know. Ask what would be welcome before deciding on a gift.
  2. Let children help choose. Give them a clear brief: the recipient’s interests, the agreed budget, and any age or material requirements. Talk about why their choice might be useful.
  3. Make a thank-you card. Invite each child to draw or write something specific they appreciate. A short, personal message can accompany a gift or stand on its own.
  4. Ask about a shared toy. A school or community group may have a use for a complete game, art supplies, or a building set. Confirm its actual needs and storage space first.
  5. Plan time to play together. If your gift is for someone nearby, offer an afternoon to use it together. If they are far away, ask whether they would enjoy sharing a story about what they made.

For children, giving becomes easier to understand when it involves a real person and a clear purpose. Ask them what they learned about the recipient while choosing. Keep the conversation open; gratitude does not need to become a comparison between children’s lives.

Sending or donating toys to Kenya: ask before you ship

A personal gift to someone you know and a donation to an organization require different arrangements. Start with the recipient in either case. Confirm that the items are wanted, how they should arrive, and who will handle delivery.

For an organizational donation, check the organization’s own instructions. Kitendo Children’s Charity in Kenya, for example, asks people to contact it before sending goods. Its published guidance explains that donated parcels can create import and handling costs and that funding local purchases can be more beneficial for its program.

That guidance belongs to that organization; it is not a description of Lisa’s shipment or a rule for every recipient. It illustrates why checking locally matters before you spend money on international postage.

  • Confirm the children’s ages, interests, and the caregiver’s requested items.
  • Keep complete sets together with their original instructions and age guidance.
  • Agree on the shipping method, total costs, and responsibility for any import charges.
  • Ask whether buying in Kenya would better meet the recipient’s needs.
  • Use the carrier’s current estimate and leave room for delays; avoid promising arrival on Thanksgiving.

Thanksgiving giving: common questions

When is Thanksgiving in the United States in 2026?

Thursday, November 26, 2026. If you are planning to send a gift overseas, ask the carrier about timing well before that date.

What are thoughtful Thanksgiving gifts for kids?

Choose around the child’s interests and the caregiver’s preferences. A complete building set, a book, drawing supplies, or a shared game can be a starting point. Spending time together or writing a personal card can also be meaningful.

Can I donate toys to children in Kenya from the U.S.?

Ask the receiving organization first. Its accepted items, delivery arrangements, and import costs will determine whether sending toys is useful. Some organizations prefer support for local purchases.

How can I teach kids gratitude at Thanksgiving?

Help them name someone they appreciate and choose a concrete way to express it. Writing a card, checking what a recipient wants, and helping with a family giving plan turn an abstract idea into something they can do.

A connection worth keeping

Lisa’s story starts with work in Kenya and continues with toys sent from America. This Thanksgiving, think about a connection in your own life that deserves a little attention. Ask what would be welcome, choose thoughtfully, and follow through.
