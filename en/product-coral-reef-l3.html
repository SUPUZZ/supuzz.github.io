BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few useful answers.

How do the three levels compare?

Coral Reef L1 has 38 pieces, L2 has 76 and L3 has 136. The L2 and L3 sets include storage boxes.

Does L3 include a box?

Yes. A storage box is included with the 136-piece L3 set.

Are all the sets in the mixed-world photos included?

No. Photos combining ocean, ice and forest themes show possible arrangements with additional sets, which are sold separately.
