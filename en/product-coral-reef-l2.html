BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few useful answers.

How do the three levels compare?

Coral Reef L1 has 38 pieces, L2 has 76 and L3 has 136. The L2 and L3 sets include storage boxes.

Does this set include a storage box?

Yes. The package-content image lists a storage box for this L2 set.

Can I combine it with other SUPUZZ sets?

Yes. The ocean, ice and forest nature collections use compatible connections. Other sets shown in mixed-world photos are sold separately.
