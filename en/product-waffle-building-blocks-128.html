HOW IT CONNECTS

Build out. Build up. Build around.

The waffle-shaped edges connect horizontally and vertically. Other pieces introduce curved and circular connections, so a flat arrangement can become a standing structure or part of a vehicle.

BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few useful answers.

Can the pieces connect in more than one direction?

Yes. The connection examples show horizontal, vertical, circular and curved arrangements, allowing both flat and three-dimensional builds.

Is the storage case included?

Yes. The 128-piece set includes a locking case for keeping the pieces together.

What can children build first?

Start with a simple shape from the project guide, then try a vehicle or character. The guide includes different levels of projects.
