Play ideas

Bring ocean, ice and forest together in one imaginative world

SUPUZZ Editorial Team · Published November 20, 2025

A tree full of fish or an icy garden beneath the sea: an imaginary scene does not have to follow the real world. Start with a child’s idea and build a story around it.

Start with connections that fit

SUPUZZ ocean, ice and forest collections include compatible nature-series connectors. Rearrange bases, stems and decorations to imagine another landscape. Not every SUPUZZ product or piece uses the same system; check the selected set and never force a connection.

Three scenes to try

Let children name the place and decide who lives there.

  • An ocean forest: imagine coral and branches as trees where fish live.
  • An icy garden: arrange winter decorations, flowers and creatures on blue bases.
  • A path between worlds: put ocean and ice scenes side by side and invent a journey.

Ask a question that opens a story

Who lives here? Where did that fish come from? Ask about the idea before correcting a shape. A story question gives children room to explain and add something new.

Find a role for everyone

One person arranges the ground, another chooses decorations and another names the characters. Build one shared scene or connect separate small worlds at the end.

Explore what you already have

You do not need several sets to begin. Reimagining a familiar piece can change the game. Before adding another set, check its contents and connection examples.

Frequently asked questions

Do nature-series pieces connect directly to waffle blocks?

The systems differ. Check each product’s instructions; separate builds can still share a story side by side.

Must we follow an example?

No. Follow the product instructions for use while inventing your own arrangement. Do not force pieces that do not fit.
