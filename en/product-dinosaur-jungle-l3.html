How does this version compare with the other sets?

L3 is the largest of these three sets, with 129 pieces. L2 contains 99 pieces and L1 contains 41.

Can the base tiles be rearranged?

Yes. The puzzle-style base tiles connect to each other so the park can be rebuilt in different shapes.
